1. Introduction

Servigic Pro ("we," "our," "us") is the Service Provider ("Pro") companion app to the Servigic home services marketplace, which connects independent tradespeople — plumbers, electricians, AC technicians, carpenters, and more — with Customers who need work done. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you use the Servigic Pro app.

By creating a Pro account, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy, including the identity verification requirements necessary to operate as a Service Provider on Servigic.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account Information

  • Full name, phone number, and email address
  • Password (stored securely, hashed — never stored in plain text)
  • Profile photo (required for Pro accounts, per our verification standards)
  • City / area of residence and service radius
  • Preferred language

2.2 Identity & Trade Verification (Pro-Specific)

Required for Pro Accounts

To keep the Servigic marketplace safe and trustworthy for Customers, all Pros must complete identity verification. We collect:

  • CNIC (national identity card) copy — used solely to verify your legal identity before you can accept jobs
  • Selfie photo — matched against your CNIC to confirm you are who you say you are
  • Police verification certificate (for higher verification tiers, where applicable) — used to unlock the "Verified Pro" badge and priority job dispatch
  • Trade/skill category and any supporting certifications you choose to upload

Verification documents are reviewed by our admin team only for the purpose of approving your Pro account and are stored securely. They are never shown to Customers or other Pros.

2.3 Job & Booking Information

  • Job details you view, bid on, and accept, including photos and descriptions provided by Customers
  • Your bid amounts, messages, and offers
  • Booking status, timestamps, and job completion details
  • Ratings and reviews (both those you receive from Customers, and those you submit about Customers)
  • In-app chat messages related to an active booking

2.4 Payout Account Information

To pay out any applicable earnings or process wallet top-ups, we collect your preferred payout method details: JazzCash number, EasyPaisa number, or bank account information. We do not store full banking credentials beyond what is necessary to identify the correct account for a transfer, and this information is only used for the purposes described in Section 5.

2.5 Device & Usage Information

  • Device type, operating system, and app version
  • IP address and general (country/city-level) location derived from it
  • App usage analytics (screens viewed, features used, crash reports)
  • Push notification tokens (to deliver job alerts and dispatch ring notifications)

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create, verify, and manage your Pro account
  • To match nearby job requests to your trade and service radius, and alert you in real time
  • To send job dispatch alerts (including the audible "incoming job" ring notification), bid updates, and booking status changes
  • To calculate, track, and settle Servigic's commission via your in-app Commission Wallet (see Section 5)
  • To verify your identity and trade credentials and maintain marketplace trust and safety
  • To enable in-app chat between you and a Customer on a confirmed booking
  • To share your live location with a Customer during an active job you have accepted (see Section 4)
  • To improve app performance, fix bugs, and develop new features
  • To detect fraud, abuse, cancellation patterns, and violations of our Terms of Service and Pro Agreement
  • To respond to support requests

4. Location Data (Including Background Location)

Background Location

Why we need it: Location access is core to how Servigic Pro works — it lets us alert you to relevant jobs near you and lets Customers see you approaching during an active job, similar to a ride-hailing driver app.

  • While you are Online (available for jobs): your approximate location is used to determine which job alerts to send you, based on your service radius. This can continue in the background (screen off or app minimized) so you don't miss incoming job alerts, and to power the audible dispatch ring even when the app is not actively open.
  • During an active, accepted booking: your precise location is shared with that specific Customer only, starting when you mark yourself "On the Way" and ending when the job is marked complete, so they can see you approaching in real time.
  • While Offline: we do not collect your location.

You can disable location permissions in your device settings at any time, but doing so will prevent you from going Online and receiving job alerts, which is core to using Servigic Pro.

5. Commission Wallet & Earnings Data

Payment Model

Servigic Pro operates on a direct-payment model: Customers pay you directly (in cash or by their own transfer) after a job is completed. Servigic does not collect or hold Customer payments.

Instead, we maintain an in-app Commission Wallet that records the commission you owe Servigic on each completed job. You settle this balance by topping up via JazzCash, EasyPaisa, or bank transfer to Servigic's business account. We collect and retain:

  • A ledger of commission owed per completed job
  • Your top-up submissions, including proof-of-payment screenshots you upload and their verification status
  • Your wallet balance and dispatch status (a wallet balance beyond a set threshold may temporarily pause new job alerts until settled, as disclosed in the app and our Pro Agreement)

This data is used solely to operate the commission system fairly and transparently, and is visible to you at all times within the app.

7. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only in these circumstances:

  • With Customers: your first name, rating, badge level, and trade category before a booking; your live location, phone number, and full name once a booking is confirmed and you mark yourself "On the Way."
  • With service providers listed in Section 6, strictly to operate the app.
  • For legal reasons: if required by law, court order, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Servigic, our users, or the public.
  • Business transfers: if Servigic is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, user information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to the same privacy protections.

Verification documents (CNIC, selfie, police certificate) are never shared with Customers or other Pros, and are only accessible to Servigic's admin team for verification purposes.

8. Data Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data, including encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS), hashed passwords, restricted admin-only access to verification documents, and role-based access controls limiting who within our team can access user data. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Data Retention

We retain account, verification, and booking information for as long as your Pro account is active, and for a reasonable period afterward to comply with legal, tax, and dispute-resolution obligations. Live location data shared during an active booking (Section 4) is retained only briefly and is not kept once a booking is completed. Background location used for job matching while Online is not retained beyond what is needed to serve you relevant, timely job alerts. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time (see Section 10).

10. Your Rights & Choices

  • Access & correction: you can view and update your profile information directly within the app.
  • Account deletion: you may request full deletion of your account and personal data — see Delete Your Account.
  • Notification preferences: you can manage push notification permissions in your device settings.
  • Location permissions: can be revoked at any time in device settings (with the functionality limitations noted in Section 4 — you will not be able to go Online or receive job alerts).

11. Children's Privacy

Servigic Pro is not directed at children and requires identity verification suitable only for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.
